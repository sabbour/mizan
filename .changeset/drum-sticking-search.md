Suggested drum sticking is now worked out for the whole part at once instead of note by note. It looks for the easiest hands overall: the right hand keeps the hi-hat or ride with the left on the snare, fast single strokes alternate (judged by seconds, so the song's tempo counts), fills travel around the kit without crossing an arm over the other, and a double stroke appears only where alternating would force a crossing. Lesson sticking is unchanged.
