Guitar and bass fretting-hand fingering now looks at the whole part instead of one grip at a time. A best-path search picks one finger per fret in a position, uses the 1-2-4 Simandl fingering on low bass frets, covers same-fret chord notes with an index barre (F major, B minor), keeps open strings fingerless, and never puts one finger on two frets. The string and fret choice also avoids needless hand shifts, the reach near the nut is shorter, and fingering written in a lesson is kept as written.
