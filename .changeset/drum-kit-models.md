Show drums on a detailed 3D kit in its own pane beside the score: an electronic or an acoustic kit, each in four looks (Realistic, Hand-painted clay, Neon rhythm and Technical blueprint). Pieces glow in the hand's colour as their notes approach and pulse when played, cymbals and the hi-hat move with the hits and pedal, and the kit stays sharp at any window size or screen density. If the graphics device cannot draw the kit, the pane says so and the named pieces keep lighting with the hits.
