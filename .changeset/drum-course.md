Add a built-in drum course: 19 offline lessons from steady pulse and rests through subdivisions, rudiments, grooves with hi-hat and ride, fills and dynamics to 3/4 and 6/8. Each lesson opens from the library in the practice view with its score, 3D kit, click, count-in and loop at a starting tempo, with its count, sticking and steps beside the score; lessons keep their own practice state apart from your songs.
