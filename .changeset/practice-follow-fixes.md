Detect changing, odd and compound time signatures from tracked downbeats and write drum parts without cluttering rests, with matching tempo in exports and a metronome that counts each bar's own pulse. While practising, the score centres the playing line and offers Scroll to start, the piano playhead follows notes inside each bar, a re-analysed song resumes inside its own bars, fast triple-time songs click on the felt beat with an unaccented pickup, and piano and drum practice views (Hands & keys, 3D kit) dock under the score.
