Guitar and bass practice can octave-fold recognized chord tones when a voicing cannot fit, preserving the chord pitch classes before marking notes unplayable. If the instrument still has too few strings, non-chord notes are reduced before chord tones. The practice notice identifies shifted tones, while the saved analysis and **As transcribed** view keep the original register. Drum sticking now assigns simultaneous cymbal strokes to both hands instead of leaving them without a suggestion.
