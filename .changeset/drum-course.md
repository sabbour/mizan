Add a built-in drum course: 37 offline lessons in eight levels, from steady pulse and rests through subdivisions, rudiments, grooves with hi-hat and ride, fills and dynamics to 3/4 and 6/8, plus 19 lessons in two style collections (inspired by great drummers, and grooves by genre). The library has Songs and Drum course tabs; each lesson opens from the Drum course tab in the practice view with its score, 3D kit, click, count-in and loop at a starting tempo, with its count, sticking (L and R in hand colours) and steps beside the score; lessons keep their own practice state apart from your songs, and count-in, click, sticking, speed and kit choice follow you between lessons.
