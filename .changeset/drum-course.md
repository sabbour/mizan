Add a built-in drum course: 37 offline lessons in eight levels, from steady pulse and rests through subdivisions, rudiments, grooves with hi-hat and ride, fills and dynamics to 3/4 and 6/8, plus two style collections: 8 lessons inspired by great drummers, and 55 grooves by genre, five for each of 11 styles (basic groove, two variations, a fill and a four-bar phrase), each played with an original bass line and chords in the style that you can mute or turn down in the track list. The library has Songs and Drum course tabs; each lesson opens from the Drum course tab in the practice view with its score, 3D kit, click, count-in and loop at a starting tempo, with its count, sticking (L and R in hand colours) and steps beside the score; lessons keep their own practice state apart from your songs, and count-in, click, sticking, speed and kit choice follow you between lessons.
